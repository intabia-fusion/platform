# Glossary

Terms that come up often in Demo's issues and team discussions.

- **Release** - an app version published to the app stores.
- **Milestone** - a group of tasks tied to a specific release.
- **Regression** - a previously fixed bug that resurfaced after a change.
- **Hotfix** - an urgent fix shipped outside the regular release schedule.
- **Catalog** - the list of products available to order in the app.
- **Onboarding** - a user's (or employee's) first introduction to the product.
